<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/square-divisors4319/1">Square Divisors</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Basic</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color);"><p data-start="1021" data-end="1102">Given a positive integer <strong>n</strong>, count the divisors of <strong>n </strong>that are perfect squares.</p>
<p data-start="1104" data-end="1148">A divisor d of n is considered valid if:</p>
<ul data-start="1150" data-end="1199">
<li data-section-id="1f48079" data-start="1150" data-end="1172">&nbsp;<strong>d </strong>divides <strong>n</strong>, and</li>
<li data-section-id="1ny0muj" data-start="1173" data-end="1199">&nbsp;<strong>d </strong>is a perfect square.</li>
</ul>
<p data-start="1201" data-end="1235">Return the count of such divisors.</p>
<p><strong><span style="font-size: 18px;">Examples:</span></strong></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong style="font-size: 18px;">Input: </strong><span style="font-size: 18px;">36
</span><strong style="font-size: 18px;">Output:</strong><span style="font-size: 18px;"> 4
</span><strong style="font-size: 18px;">Explaination:</strong><span style="font-size: 18px;"> The factors which are perfect square are 1, 4, 9 and 36.<br></span></span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input:</strong> 60
<strong>Output:</strong> 2
<strong>Explaination:</strong> The factors which are perfect squares are 1 and 4.</span></span></pre>
